Historical: EB Garamond and IM Fell English

For a historical novel in paperback, set the body in EB Garamond at 11/14.5 pt and chapter titles in IM Fell English, on a 5.5 × 8.5 in trim. An old-style body with a rougher display face that recalls early printing.

About this preview

Your browser sets the preview, so line breaks and hyphens can fall a little differently from the PDF your formatting software makes. The fonts, sizes and margins are the ones in the table.

The margins assume a book of about 300 pages, for which KDP asks for an inside margin of at least 0.5 in (12.7 mm). For your own page count, use the KDP cover calculator.

Both fonts are under the SIL Open Font License; the templates page says what it allows for books.
