Sci-Fi: Literata and Space Grotesk

For a fantasy or science fiction novel in paperback, set the body in Literata at 10.5/14 pt and chapter titles in Space Grotesk, on a 5.5 × 8.5 in trim. A sturdy, open body text with a clean geometric heading that reads as modern.

Paste from Word, or leave a blank line between paragraphs. *Asterisks* or _underscores_ make italics. A line of *** is a scene break. Your text stays in this browser and follows you to every template.

About this preview

Your browser sets the preview, so line breaks and hyphens can fall a little differently from the PDF your formatting software makes. The fonts, sizes and margins are the ones in the table.

The margins assume a book of about 300 pages, for which KDP asks for an inside margin of at least 0.5 in (12.7 mm). For your own page count, use the KDP cover calculator.

The SIL Open Font License (OFL) allows you to use these fonts freely in print publications and e-books, even if you sell them. Referencing or embedding an OFL font in a document does not change the license of the document itself.
