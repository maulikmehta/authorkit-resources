A KDP paperback cover is one PDF: back, spine and front, with 0.125″ of bleed on every outside edge. Its size depends on your trim, your paper and your page count. Pick them below. The interior margins for the same page count are shown too, so the cover and the inside of the book agree.

How the full cover is measured

Width = bleed + back + spine + front + bleed. Height = bleed + trim height + bleed. KDP prints even page counts, so an odd count is rounded up before the spine is measured. Spine text is allowed only on books with more than 79 pages.
