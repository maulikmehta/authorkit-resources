How the full cover is measured

A KDP paperback cover is one continuous PDF spread consisting of the back cover, spine and front cover, with 0.125 inches (3.2 mm) of bleed added to every outside edge. Full width equals bleed plus back cover width plus spine width plus front cover width plus bleed. Full height equals trim height plus bleed.

KDP prints even page counts only. An odd page count is rounded up to the next even integer before the spine is measured. Spine text is permitted only on books with 80 pages or more. In the diagram, the outer hatched band is the bleed trimmed during manufacturing, the solid line marks the final trim edge, the dashed lines indicate spine folds, and the rectangular box on the back cover marks the 2.0 × 1.2 inch area reserved for the barcode.

KDP spine width calculator formula and limits

Amazon KDP calculates spine thickness by multiplying the page count by paper thickness. Each paper type has an exact multiplier from KDP specifications:

Black and white interior on white paper: page count × 0.002252 inches (0.0572 mm).

Black and white interior on cream paper: page count × 0.0025 inches (0.0635 mm).

Black and white interior on groundwood paper: page count × 0.00235 inches (0.0597 mm).

Standard color interior on white paper: page count × 0.002252 inches (0.0572 mm).

Premium color interior on white paper: page count × 0.002347 inches (0.0596 mm).

KDP requires an even page count. Odd numbers round up to the next even integer before spine calculation. Amazon allows spine text only on books with 80 pages or more. Books with 79 pages or fewer must leave the spine blank. The spine text must also sit at least 0.0625 inches (1.6 mm) inside the spine fold lines on both sides.

How to use these dimensions in Canva

Follow these steps to set up your KDP cover in Canva:

  1. Create a custom canvas. In Canva, click Create a design and choose Custom size. Set the unit dropdown to px.
  2. Enter the 300 DPI pixel dimensions. Copy the width and height shown in the Canva box above and paste them into the width and height fields. Click Create new design.
  3. Upload the guide PNG. Click Download template guide (PNG) above. In Canva, upload this PNG and stretch it to cover the entire canvas exactly. Lock the guide layer or reduce its transparency while you arrange your front cover, spine and back cover art.
  4. Keep text inside the safe margins. Keep titles, barcodes and author names away from the outer 0.125-inch bleed zone and inside the safe margin guides. Background artwork must extend all the way to the canvas edges.
  5. Export as PDF Print. Before exporting, delete or hide the template guide layer. Click Share, choose Download, select PDF Print, check Flatten PDF, and download your print-ready file.

How to use it
  1. Match your KDP setup. Choose the same paper and trim you picked on KDP's paperback content page.
  2. Enter the final page count. Use the page count of your finished interior PDF, not a draft.
  3. Make your cover canvas the full cover size. Put the back on the left and the front on the right, and let background art run into the bleed.
  4. Check against KDP's template. Before you upload, download KDP's own template for the same settings and compare.

If the page count changes, the spine changes, and so does the cover. The interior gutter in the results is the inside margin for the same page count.
