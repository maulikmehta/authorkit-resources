How to use it
  1. Export your final PDF. Check the file you will upload, not a draft.
  2. Open it in a PDF viewer. The document properties show page size and fonts; scrolling shows images, blank pages and margins.
  3. Tick each item you have confirmed. Every item quotes KDP and links to the page it comes from.

Your ticks stay in this browser only. KDP's own Print Previewer, after upload, is the final check.
