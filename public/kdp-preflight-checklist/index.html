KDP paperback preflight checklist

Before you upload a paperback interior PDF, tick through what KDP rejects or warns about: page count, page size, margins and more, each quoted from KDP's help.

Your PDF has at least the minimum and no more than the maximum pages for your paper, ink and trim.

KDP: “The minimum page count is 24 pages, and the maximum page count depends on ink, paper, and trim size options.” Paperback submission guidelines

Every page is exactly your trim size, or your trim size plus bleed if your book has bleed.

KDP: “Set the page size to your selected trim size (width by height).” Paperback submission guidelines

Nothing you need to keep sits closer to the outside edges, or to the spine, than the margins for your page count (shown above once you enter it).

KDP: “The top, bottom, and outside margins must be a minimum of 0.25 inches for books without bleed and 0.375 inches (9.6mm) for books with bleed. The inside margin size depends on the page count” Trim, bleed and margins

Every image is embedded in your file, not linked. Page through the PDF and check each picture appears.

KDP: “Embed all fonts and images in your native file prior to submission.” Paperback submission guidelines

In your PDF viewer’s document properties, every font is listed as embedded.

KDP: “All fonts in the interior files should be embedded in the native program before publishing.” Paperback submission guidelines

Each image, at the size it is printed, has enough pixels to print sharp.

KDP: “Minimum resolution of 300 DPI.” Paperback submission guidelines

If any image, background or illustration should reach the edge of a page, the whole file is sized for bleed.

KDP: “You should include bleed in your interior file if you have any images, backgrounds, or illustrations in your book that you want to reach the edge of the page.” Trim, bleed and margins

Count blank pages in a row. The limit is lower at the start and middle of the book than at the end.

KDP: “we allow no more than 4 consecutive blank pages at the beginning or middle of a manuscript file, and/or 10 consecutive blank pages at the end.” Fix paperback and hardcover formatting issues

Images and backgrounds meant to touch the edge run past the trim line on the top, bottom and outside edges.

KDP: “extend them 0.125" (3.2 mm) beyond the final trim size from the top, bottom, and outer edges of your manuscript.” Paperback submission guidelines

The PDF has no printer’s marks, and no bookmarks, comments or placeholder text.

KDP: “Submitted files should not contain crop marks, trim marks, bookmarks, comments, invisible objects, annotations, placeholder text, or metadata.” Paperback submission guidelines

Each PDF page is one book page, not two facing pages side by side.

KDP: “We require single page files (as opposed to spreads or 2-up files).” Paperback submission guidelines

Check the PDF’s size on disk against KDP’s limit.

KDP: “Ensure file size is not more than 650MB.” Paperback submission guidelines

How to use it
  1. Export your final PDF. Check the file you will upload, not a draft.
  2. Open it in a PDF viewer. The document properties show page size and fonts; scrolling shows images, blank pages and margins.
  3. Tick each item you have confirmed. Every item quotes KDP and links to the page it comes from.

Your ticks stay in this browser only. KDP's own Print Previewer, after upload, is the final check.
