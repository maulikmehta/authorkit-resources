How KDP computes the royalty

For a paperback sold on Amazon.com, KDP pays 60% of the list price minus the printing cost when the list price is $9.99 or more, and 50% minus the printing cost below that: (royalty rate × list price) − printing cost = royalty. The printing cost itself is a fixed cost plus a per-page cost that depends on your ink, paper and trim size.

Expanded Distribution pays 40% of the list price minus the printing cost, and makes the paperback available to bookstores and libraries outside Amazon. At a low list price this royalty can be zero or less. When it is, the calculator shows Expanded Distribution as not available at that price.

This calculator covers Amazon.com paperback sales. Authors selling through other Amazon marketplaces can verify local print schedules on KDP Help.

KDP printing cost calculator formulas

Amazon KDP calculates paperback printing costs using a fixed charge plus a per-page rate based on ink, paper choice and trim size.

Standard trim sizes (6 × 9 inches and smaller)

Standard trims include 5 × 8, 5.25 × 8, 5.5 × 8.5 and 6 × 9 inches, where width is 6.12 inches or less and height is 9.0 inches or less.

Black and white interior on cream or white paper costs a fixed $2.30 for books with 24 to 108 pages. Books with 110 pages or more cost $1.00 fixed plus $0.012 per page.

Black and white on groundwood paper costs a fixed $2.23 for 24 to 112 pages. Above 112 pages, the formula is $1.00 fixed plus $0.0114 per page.

Standard color on white paper costs $1.00 fixed plus $0.0255 per page across all page counts (minimum 72 pages).

Premium color on white paper costs a fixed $3.60 for 24 to 40 pages. Above 40 pages, the formula is $1.00 fixed plus $0.065 per page.

Large trim sizes (larger than 6 × 9 inches)

Large trims include 7 × 10, 8 × 10 and 8.5 × 11 inches, where width exceeds 6.12 inches or height exceeds 9.0 inches.

Black and white interior on cream or white paper costs a fixed $2.84 for 24 to 108 pages, or $1.00 fixed plus $0.017 per page above 108 pages.

Black and white on groundwood paper costs a fixed $2.75 for 24 to 112 pages, or $1.00 fixed plus $0.0162 per page above 112 pages.

Standard color costs $1.00 fixed plus $0.0402 per page.

Premium color costs a fixed $4.20 for 24 to 40 pages, or $1.00 fixed plus $0.080 per page above 40 pages.

Amazon KDP ebook royalty rates and delivery costs

Amazon offers two royalty plans for Kindle ebooks: 35% and 70%.

The 35% royalty plan applies to list prices between $0.99 and $200.00. Amazon deducts no delivery fees on the 35% plan: Royalty = 35% × List Price.

The 70% royalty plan applies to list prices between $2.99 and $9.99 in qualifying sales territories (including the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Germany). On the 70% plan, Amazon deducts digital delivery costs of $0.15 per megabyte based on your ebook file size: Royalty = 70% × (List Price − Delivery Cost).

Sales outside the $2.99 to $9.99 price window earn 35%. Ebook sales to readers in India on Amazon.in require enrollment in KDP Select to qualify for the 70% royalty tier.

This calculator models paperback printing costs and royalties. It does not calculate digital delivery deductions for ebook file sizes.

US withholding for an Indian author

KDP withholds US tax from royalties by default at 30%. An author who completes the W-8BEN tax interview in KDP and doesn't have a US taxpayer ID can enter the income tax identification number issued by the tax authority in their country of residence instead. Under the India-US tax treaty (Article 12), the rate on royalties for the copyright of a literary, artistic or scientific work is 15%. A book counts as a literary work, so this calculator uses 15%.

The tax withheld in the US isn't the end of it: under Indian tax rules, a resident who has already paid tax on foreign income can claim credit for it using Form 67 when filing their Indian return, so the same royalty isn't taxed twice.

This is not tax advice. Indian income tax on royalties is calculated separately from this US withholding; talk to a tax professional about your own return.
