Novel word count by genre

Word counts for standard fiction-length categories and a few publishers' genre lines, each from a source checked today — not every genre is covered.

CategoryWord countSource
Short story (SFWA)1–7,499 wordsSFWA (checked 2026-09-29)
Novelette (SFWA)7,500–17,499 wordsSFWA (checked 2026-09-29)
Novella (SFWA)17,500–39,999 wordsSFWA (checked 2026-09-29)
Novel (SFWA)40,000 or more wordsSFWA (checked 2026-09-29)
Adult science fiction & fantasy (Baen Books)100,000–130,000 wordsBaen Books (checked 2026-09-29)
Historical romance (Harlequin Historical)70,000–75,000 wordsHarlequin (checked 2026-09-29)
Inspirational romance, trade (Love Inspired Trade)85,000–100,000 wordsHarlequin (checked 2026-09-29)

Why some genres aren't listed

Every range above comes from a source that states an exact word count and was reachable today: an awards body's own length definitions, or a publisher's or imprint's submission guidelines. Genres people often ask about — literary fiction, mystery and thriller, historical fiction, young adult, middle grade, picture books, general fantasy and horror — aren't shown here, because a search today turned up only blog posts and "industry standard" figures with no named source behind them, not a publisher's or agency's own stated number. A range that can't be traced to one of those is left off rather than guessed at.

Romance shows two separate Harlequin lines rather than one blended "romance" figure, because Harlequin's own submission guidelines give a different word count for each line, not one number for the whole imprint.

Sources (last verified 2026-09-29):

  • SFWA: Complete Nebula Awards Rules: short story, novelette, novella and novel length definitions
  • Baen Books: submission guidelines: preferred length for adult science fiction and fantasy novels
  • Harlequin: submission guidelines: word count for the Harlequin Historical and Love Inspired Trade lines
